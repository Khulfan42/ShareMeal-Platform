Web-Based Centralized Platform That Optimizes Food Waste Redistribution by Linking Restaurants with Registered Charitable Organizations

🌱 Final Year Project (FYP) • BSCS Session 2024–2026 • ShareMeal Ecosystem
📋

Description

Food wastage is one of the most pressing socio-economic challenges in Pakistan, where millions endure severe nutritional deprivation while banquet halls, hotels, and restaurants discard metric tons of edible surplus food daily. The ShareMeal Platform is an enterprise web-based centralized solution engineered using ASP.NET Core 8.0, C# 12, Entity Framework Core, and Google Gemini AI. The system provides real-time digital linking between commercial surplus food donors and vetted charitable welfare NGOs. By digitizing food inventory listings, automating pickup dispatching, enforcing strict food safety time-windows, and providing AI-powered operational assistance, ShareMeal bridges the critical logistics gap to eradicate food waste and feed impoverished communities sustainably.

🎯

Scope of the System

  • Nationwide Cloud Accessibility: Responsive web platform functioning seamlessly across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices without requiring app store installation.
  • Multi-Role Role-Based Architecture: Segregated, secure portals for Food Donors (Restaurants), Claiming Charities (NGOs), and System Administrators.
  • Real-Time Food Listing & Matching: Dynamic surplus inventory listing containing meal portions, preparation timestamp, preservation state, and expiry countdown.
  • Atomic Concurrency Claim Engine: Database-level transaction locking preventing duplicate claims or race conditions between competing charities.
  • Bilingual Multimodal Gemini AI (MealBot): 24/7 intelligent voice-enabled chatbot providing automated SOP instructions, platform navigation, and meal safety assistance.
  • Interactive Geolocation Mapping: Integration with Leaflet.js and GPS routing for pinpointing donor addresses, transit estimation, and distribution verification.
⚙️

Functional Requirements

  • User registration, login, and profile management with hashed credentials (ASP.NET Core Identity).
  • Restaurant / Donor Module: Add, edit, manage, and monitor surplus food listings with expiry deadlines.
  • Charity / NGO Module: Real-time marketplace feed, location filtering, instant donation claim reservation.
  • Strict Verification Pipeline: Admin approval workflows verifying government NGO credentials and NTN.
  • Status Lifecycle Engine: Automated transitions across Available → Claimed → In-Transit → Delivered.
  • Food Safety & Hygiene SOPs: Mandatory pre-claim verification of preparation time and food temperature.
  • MealBot AI Integration: Natural language processing in Roman Urdu, Urdu, and English with Web Speech API.
  • System Analytics & Reporting: Comprehensive PDF and visual telemetry for meals saved and carbon offset.
